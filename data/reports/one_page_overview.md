# Procedural Intelligence Layer v1.1 — Overview

This repository provides structured legal procedure datasets for:

- India
- UAE
- UK
- Saudi Arabia (KSA)

Across four domains:
- Criminal
- Civil
- Family
- Consumer / Commercial

## What This Is
A procedural intelligence layer describing how legal processes move through authorities, decisions, and outcomes.

## What This Is Not
- Legal advice
- Predictive judgment engine
- Strategy or recommendation system

## Key Features
- Step-by-step procedural flows
- Conditional branching at decision points
- Outcome intelligence with safe probability bands
- Cost, effort, and difficulty metadata
- Risk and evidence sensitivity flags

## Intended Use
- Legal AI reasoning engines
- Agent-based simulations
- User-facing procedural guidance
- Internal legal process mapping

All data is factual, jurisdiction-correct, and source-driven.
