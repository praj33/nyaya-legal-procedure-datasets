# Procedural Intelligence Gap Analysis (v1.0 → v1.1)

## Current v1.0 Capabilities
- Linear procedural steps
- Authorities and jurisdictions
- Mandatory documents
- Timeline ranges
- Escalation paths

## Missing for Decision-Grade Reasoning
- Conditional procedural branching
- Outcome differentiation
- Probability ranges for outcomes
- Cost and effort indicators
- Case difficulty scaling
- Procedural risk flags
- Evidence sensitivity awareness

## Impact of Missing Elements
- AI cannot simulate alternative procedural paths
- RL systems lack structured reward signals
- UI cannot represent decision states
- Judicial guidance lacks outcome framing

## Resolution Approach
Introduce additive, optional intelligence layers
at step-level without modifying or breaking
existing procedural structure.
