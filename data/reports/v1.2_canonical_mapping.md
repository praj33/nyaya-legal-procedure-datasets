# v1.2 Canonical Normalization Mapping

This report documents the introduction of canonical procedural
identifiers across jurisdictions.

## Purpose
Enable cross-jurisdiction reasoning without altering
jurisdiction-specific legal procedures.

## Scope
- Criminal, Civil, Family, Consumer / Commercial
- India, UAE, UK, KSA

## Method
Canonical tags were added as additive metadata to existing
procedural steps and outcomes. No procedural logic was modified.

## Result
All major procedural decision points now map to shared
canonical identifiers, enabling normalization for AI reasoning
and reinforcement learning layers.
